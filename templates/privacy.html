Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy describes how CardWapas collects, uses, and protects information when you register an account, add a metro card, or report a found card. Read it carefully before using the service.

Note: CardWapas is a student hackathon project. This policy describes the actual implementation and is not a lawyer-reviewed document. If you have questions, contact the team at the address listed at the bottom.

1. Information we collect

Account registration (card owners only)

  • Full name
  • Email address (used to send found-card alerts)
  • Phone number (optional, stored but never shown to finders or encoded in QR codes)
  • Hashed password (we never store your password in plain text)

Card records

  • A short label you choose for each card (e.g. "Blue Line Card")
  • A randomly generated recovery token, which is embedded in the QR code URL
  • Card status (active, lost, or recovered)
  • Date and time the card was registered

Found reports (submitted by finders)

  • Proposed meetup location (chosen from a preset list)
  • Optional timing note
  • Optional message to the card owner
  • Optional contact information voluntarily provided by the finder
  • Optional GPS coordinates, if the finder chooses to share them
  • A randomly generated report token used to give the finder a private status link

2. How we use your information

  • To send you an email when a finder reports your card found.
  • To display found reports on your owner dashboard so you can coordinate a meetup.
  • To allow the finder to check the status of their report through a private, unguessable link.
  • To show accurate statistics (card count, report count, recovery count) on your dashboard.

3. What finders can and cannot see

The public finder page (/found/<token>) and the report status page (/report/<token>) are designed so that:

  • Finders are never shown the owner's name, email address, or phone number.
  • The QR code URL contains only a random token. It does not reveal any personal details.
  • Finders can see the owner's reply message if the owner chooses to send one.

4. Email notifications

When a finder submits a report, the application sends an email to the registered owner. Email delivery uses SMTP (configured via environment variables). If no mail credentials are configured in the deployment environment, notification emails are printed to the server console only and are not delivered.

We do not use any third-party email marketing service. Emails are sent only as a direct result of a found-card report.

5. Data storage and security

  • Data is stored in a SQLite database on the hosting server.
  • Passwords are hashed using Werkzeug's generate_password_hash function.
  • All POST forms are protected with a CSRF token.
  • HTTPS is recommended and should be enforced in production via the hosting provider.
  • Limitation: As a hackathon prototype, this application has not undergone a formal security audit. Do not store sensitive personal information beyond what is required for card recovery.

6. Data retention and deletion

Card owners can delete their cards and all associated found reports at any time from the dashboard. Account deletion is not currently implemented in the interface; contact the team if you need your account removed.

The team should confirm and document a specific retention period here before a public launch.

7. Third-party services

  • Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons are loaded from the jsDelivr CDN. Your browser will connect to jsDelivr's servers to download these assets.
  • If GPS coordinates are shared and the owner views the map link, the browser connects to OpenStreetMap to display the location.
  • No advertising, analytics, or social-media tracking scripts are used.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting the team using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
