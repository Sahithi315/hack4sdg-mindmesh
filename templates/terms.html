You must be old enough to enter into agreements under the laws of your country. By using CardWapas, you confirm that you are eligible to do so.

2. Your account

  • You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure.
  • Do not share your password or allow others to access your account.
  • The information you provide at registration (name, email) must be accurate.
  • Notify the team promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

3. Using QR codes and recovery tokens

  • Each QR code is uniquely linked to one metro card in your account.
  • Do not attach the same QR sticker to multiple cards.
  • If a QR sticker is damaged, misprinted, or shared inappropriately, use the Regenerate QR option on your dashboard. The old sticker will stop working immediately.
  • Do not place the QR code on property you do not own.

4. Acceptable use

You must not use CardWapas to:

  • Submit false or fraudulent found-card reports.
  • Harass, deceive, or attempt to extract personal information from card owners.
  • Use the finder report form to send spam or unsolicited messages.
  • Attempt to access another user's account, cards, or reports.
  • Reverse-engineer the recovery tokens or attempt to guess valid token values.
  • Disrupt the service or overload the server with automated requests.

5. Meetup safety

  • All meetups should take place in public, well-lit locations during daylight hours.
  • CardWapas provides a preset list of safe meetup locations to guide the finder. Use them.
  • Never agree to a meetup in an isolated or private location with a stranger.
  • CardWapas is not responsible for the outcome of any meetup arranged through the service.

6. What CardWapas does not do

  • CardWapas facilitates contact between finders and owners. It cannot guarantee that a lost card will be returned.
  • CardWapas does not integrate with metro operator systems (such as BMRCL). It cannot freeze a card's balance, block the card at metro gates, or track its physical location.
  • CardWapas does not verify the identity of finders or owners.
